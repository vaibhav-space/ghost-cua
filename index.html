Temporal Multimodal Audio/Video

Deep Continuous Video & Audio QA

The Brute Force Problem: Claude can only inspect 1 or 2 static image frames. It cannot evaluate full continuous video pacing, dialogue cadence, or audio-visual synchronization.

Claude delegates the video file via gemini_query. GhostCUA dispatches a process-targeted Carbon AppleEvent directly to Gemini. Gemini's video foundation model watches the complete footage and returns second-by-second timeline review.

claude "Audit pacing and audio sync of demo.mp4"
→ GhostCUA: Process-targeted aevt/odoc
Extracted detailed scene critique in 27s
